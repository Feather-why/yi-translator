Why local translation matters

Running a translation model on your own laptop means your reading never leaves the machine. That is the first reason people choose local models.

The second reason is speed: a small model on a modern graphics card can return a paragraph in about a hundred milliseconds, faster than most network requests.

The third reason is cost. There are no per-character fees, so you can translate long documents and whole websites without watching a quota.
