Doctoral researcher in coastal ecology

The research team studies coastal habitats. Read the eligibility requirements before applying.

Responsibilities

The researcher will coordinate field observations and present findings at regular team meetings.

The project brings field researchers and laboratory staff together to compare observations collected at different sites. The successful applicant will publish reproducible results, document the sampling process and share the findings with students.

Applicants should describe a question they would like to investigate and explain how the available data could help answer it. The group provides supervised laboratory access and training in methods for analysing measurements from coastal surveys.
